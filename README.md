# 🎵 Spotify Promo Workflow
### by [Harmony Hype Verdict](https://www.instagram.com/harmonyhypeverdict/) & [Maluscomas](https://www.instagram.com/maluscomas/)

A free, self-contained HTML tool to help independent musicians plan and execute a Spotify promotion campaign — no sign-up, no install, no backend. Just download and open in any browser.

## ✨ Features

- **Pitch Generator** — Instantly generate a personalized curator outreach email based on your artist name, track, genre, mood, and bio
- **5-Week Promo Plan** — A structured week-by-week action plan covering pre-release outreach, Spotify editorial submission, social content strategy, and post-release follow-up
- **Global Outreach Tracker** — 180+ contacts across blogs, magazines, radio stations, Spotify playlists, and editorial channels covering 24 genres
- **Status Tracking** — Track each outreach contact through stages: Not Started → Drafted → Sent → Followed Up → Got Response → Placed
- **No dependencies** — Single `.html` file, works offline, opens in any browser

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Download** `index.html`
2. **Open** it in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox — no install needed)
3. Go to the **Setup tab** and fill in your artist name, track name, genre, mood, release date, and bio
4. Hit **✦ Generate Pitch** to get a ready-to-send curator email
5. Hit **📅 Build Promo Plan** to generate your personalised 5-week campaign
6. Hit **📋 Load Curators** to populate the outreach tracker with genre-specific contacts
7. Track your outreach status and add notes for each contact
